## Material source findings confirmed in this pass

### BSEC Corporate Governance Code

- The operative instrument is the **Corporate Governance Code, 2018**, not a standalone “Code 2023”. It is made under s.2CC and its opening scope expressly applies to companies listed with a Bangladesh stock exchange. The 16 October 2023 Gazette notification amends Condition 1(2) (independent directors), including the minimum two-or-one-fifth formula and other specified matters. The 4 April 2024 notification amends Condition 1(2)(a) again by adding at least one female independent director. [2018 Code](https://sec.gov.bd/slaws/Corporate_Governance_Code_10.06.2018.pdf); [2023 amendment](https://sec.gov.bd/slaws/Notification_28.11.2023.pdf); [2024 amendment](https://sec.gov.bd/slaws/Notification_26.05.2024.pdf).
- Condition 5 is the Audit Committee. Condition 8 is maintaining a company website. Condition 9 is reporting and obtaining an annual corporate-governance compliance certificate. Therefore BSEC-001 (para 8 quarterly report), BSEC-002 (para 5 certificate), BSEC-003 (para 5 board composition) have incorrect condition references; BSEC-004 cites the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act for an audit committee and is plainly misanchored. [Official Code, conditions 5, 8 and 9](https://sec.gov.bd/slaws/Corporate_Governance_Code_10.06.2018.pdf).
- For NLC's private-limited-company product, BSEC rules must be disabled unless the relevant issuer actually falls within the Code's scope (e.g., listed-company status or another express BSEC instrument). Do not infer applicability from corporate form alone.

### VAT thresholds and returns

- The NBR's older FAQ still says BDT 80 lakh registration and BDT 30 lakh turnover-tax enlistment. That FAQ is stale on registration. The controlling amendment is the **Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty (Amendment) Act, 2026 (Act No. 77 of 2026), dated 10 April 2026**, which is deemed effective from **9 January 2025**. It amends the Act's definitions to set the enlistment threshold at **BDT 30 lakh** and registration threshold at **BDT 50 lakh** per 12-month period. The consolidated authentic VAT Act text (published 13 November 2025) shows those figures and the 2026 amendment Act ratifies/replaces the 2025 Ordinance. The Act also contains mandatory-registration categories under s.4(2), irrespective of turnover. Do not use the stale FAQ as the threshold authority. [BDLaws VAT Amendment Act 2026](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-1706.html); [Authentic VAT Act](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/upload/act/2026-01-18-15-14-21-72.-Value-Added-Tax-and-Supplementary-Duty-Act%2C-2012.pdf), definition clauses (48)/(57), ss.4 and 10; [NBR VAT FAQ](https://nbr.gov.bd/faq/vat-faq/eng) is visibly inconsistent and must not override the Act.
- The Act defines a return by reference to a tax period. No general annual VAT return duty was found in the consolidated Act text in this pass. VAT-003 must remain disabled unless a specific applicable rule/SRO/order establishes an annual filing obligation for this taxpayer class and period.
- VAT-002 must test registration/enlistment separately, identify the actual taxpayer class and tax period, and not assume every private company is automatically a VAT registrant solely because it exists.

### Income tax — Tax Day

- The authentic Income Tax Act 2023 text defines a company's Tax Day as the 15th day of the seventh month after the income year ends, or 15 September where that seventh-month date falls before 15 September; the next working day applies if the specified day is a public holiday. Section 171 requires filing on or before Tax Day. The rule's cited s.75 is not the correct anchor for the filing duty. [NBR authentic Income Tax Act 2023](https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/Income_tax_act_2023.pdf), s.2(23) and s.171.
- NBR lists a Finance Act 2026 dated 6 July 2026. The current effective text and any later tax-specific amendments/extensions must be reconciled before a production deadline is computed. No Tax Day value should be inferred from financial year-end alone without the actual income-year end, taxpayer classification and any valid extension.

### Labour Act — Section 326

- The current amendment is the **Bangladesh Labour (Amendment) Act, 2026 (Act No. 43 of 2026), dated 10 April 2026**. It repealed the 2025 Labour (Amendment) Ordinance. The amendment to Labour Act s.326(1)(b) authorises the Government to direct licensing for an establishment, factory or class of factory; licence renewal is every five years, while contractor-agency licence term is one year. This is not a blanket licence requirement for every private company or every establishment. LBR-001/002's s.35 anchor is wrong; keep them conditional on an applicable Government direction and establishment classification. [BDLaws 2026 Amendment Act](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-1672.html); [Ministry of Labour legislative register](https://mole.gov.bd/pages/legislative-informations).
