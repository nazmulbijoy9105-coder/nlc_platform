## Mandatory unresolved controls

- **AGM-001:** canonical engine and current seed basis is Companies Act 1994, s.81(1). Migrations `0125_reconcile_agm001_statutory_basis` and `0128_agm001_calendar_month_definition` reconcile the known legacy s.81 / 548-day catalogue definition to s.81(1) / 18 calendar months and preserve before-images. The source workpack retains the legacy seed citation for provenance. This is a technical reconciliation, **not counsel approval**. The 18-calendar-month interpretation is tested; General Clauses Act 1897, s.3(33) supports calendar-month meaning, while month-end clamping and day-count inclusion still require counsel confirmation.
- **Time computation:** General Clauses Act 1897, s.9 contains default “from”/“to” counting rules and s.10 addresses acts or proceedings to be done in a court or office on a day that is closed. Whether s.10 applies to the holding of an AGM, and the precise day-inclusion rule for s.81(1), remain counsel questions; do not automatically extend an AGM deadline for an office closure.
- **Amendment completeness:** all 85 operative intervals remain unresolved until clause-level amendment, commencement, savings, transition, repeal and corrigendum research is signed off. A primary Act commencement date is not a substitute for the commencement date of a later amendment.
- **Foreign exchange source currency:** Bangladesh Bank FEPD-2 Circular No.09 dated 29 October 2026 states that GFET-2018 Vol.1 and subsequent foreign-exchange-management instructions stand repealed on issuance, except monthly reporting instructions in GFET Vol.2; the circular states one-year validity. FX-001 therefore cannot rely on GFET-2018 Vol.1 as current authority without mapping the new circular and checking later instructions. The circular is issued under FERA 1947, s.20(3). <https://www.bb.org.bd/mediaroom/circulars/fepd-2/oct292026fepd-209e.pdf>
- **Known source conflicts:** BSEC-004 records FERA 1947 while its canonical source claim points to the BSEC Corporate Governance Code; BSEC-001–004 lack exact provision candidates. LBR-001/LBR-002 catalogue bases cite s.35 while canonical candidates include s.326(1)(b) and Labour Rules 2015 rr.354–355; the 2025 Labour Amendment Ordinance was repealed by the 2026 Amendment Act. These remain conflict/review items, not silent corrections.
- **RJSC scope:** versioned RJSC-only scope is separate from statutory validity and applicability. Non-RJSC tax, VAT, labour, BSEC, foreign-exchange, local-government or contractual obligations must not enter the RJSC score, band, rescue trigger, hash or release path without an explicit versioned scope decision and authority.
- **Historical snapshots:** `legal_rule_revisions` supports effective-dated definitions, but migration `0116` creates the table without seeding approved revision rows, and no complete binding from each company/snapshot to the exact legal source revision is yet demonstrated. Per-evaluation findings now preserve the engine `rule_version`, `scope_version`, and an explicit `NOT_BOUND_TO_EFFECTIVE_DATED_LEGAL_REVISION` status with a null legal source revision ID. ## Release gate
