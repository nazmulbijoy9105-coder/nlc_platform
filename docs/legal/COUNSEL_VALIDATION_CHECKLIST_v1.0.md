## Rule-by-rule review
- [ ] Obtain official consolidated statute and every amending Act/Ordinance/SRO/Gazette through review date.
- [ ] Record commencement, transitional and repeal provisions.
- [ ] Pin exact section/subsection/rule/paragraph and official source URL.
- [ ] Verify legal proposition and consequence, not just citation.
- [ ] Determine company-type, sector, listing, foreign-investment and transaction scope.
- [ ] Review actual Memorandum and Articles and amendments.
- [ ] Review court orders, RJSC certified records and company evidence.
- [ ] Separate VERIFIED_ABSENT from NOT_PRODUCED, UNKNOWN and REGISTRY_UNAVAILABLE.
- [ ] Verify evidence authenticity, identity, date, provenance and chain of custody.
- [ ] Verify deadline start event, calendar arithmetic, holidays, extension and condonation.
- [ ] Confirm remedy, penalty, limitation, appeal and procedural preconditions.
- [ ] Approve legal interpretation and exceptions.
- [ ] Separately approve NLC severity, score impact and BLACK override policy.
- [ ] Independently approve test vectors and negative cases.
- [ ] Sign status VALIDATED / VALIDATED_WITH_LIMITS / REJECTED / NOT_EVALUATED.
- [ ] Set next review date and amendment-monitoring owner.

## Rule statuses
- NOT_EVALUATED: not approved; must not produce a legal conclusion.
- VALIDATED_WITH_LIMITS: only the signed scope may be activated.
- VALIDATED: source, interpretation, applicability and evidence predicates approved.
- REJECTED: do not activate; preserve rejection rationale and history.
