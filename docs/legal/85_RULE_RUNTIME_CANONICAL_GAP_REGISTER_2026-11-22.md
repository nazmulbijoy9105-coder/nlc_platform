## 2. Status vocabulary

- **PRESENT** means a corresponding code/data object was located.
- **CANDIDATE** means a proposed source, fact, exception or evidence mapping exists but is not independently authenticated or legally approved.
- **ABSENT** means no corresponding entry was found in the inspected registry/file; it does not prove that the law has no such condition.
- **UNKNOWN / OPEN** means the available record does not establish the proposition.
- **NOT_APPLICABLE** may be returned only when rule-specific legal scope and facts establish non-applicability. Missing facts are not non-applicability.
- **VERIFIED_ABSENT** requires an authoritative, appropriately scoped verification; no-upload/no-record in NLC is not enough.

## 3. Cross-cutting defects and controls

1. **Runtime-to-canonical parity is unproven.** All 85 identity tokens were located in the production engine source, but this scan does not prove the same trigger, exception, evidence state, deadline and consequence across runtime and canonical paths.
2. **Applicability coverage is partial.** The typed applicability registry has 41 entries, while the workpack lists 41 applicability mappings. Every unmodelled rule must remain NOT_EVALUATED or be explicitly reviewed; do not assume universal private-company applicability.
3. **Evidence coverage is partial.** The typed evidence registry has 43 entries. Workpack candidate roles do not establish issuer authentication or evidentiary sufficiency.
4. **Exception coverage is not exhaustive.** Empty exception arrays are not legal proof that no exceptions, relief, extensions or procedural bars exist.
5. **Consequences are not sufficiently structured.** NLC severity/score fields must not stand in for statutory penalties, personal liability, invalidity, Registrar action, court remedies or regulatory consequences.
6. **Source chronology remains open.** The workpack marks amendment completeness open/non-exhaustive and rule-specific operative intervals unresolved.
7. **RJSC is not the whole compliance universe.** Tax, VAT, BSEC, labour, foreign exchange, local licensing and other conditional regimes need independent scope and source review. Their presence in the 85-rule catalogue does not establish complete domain coverage.
8. **No OCR assumption.** This register treats structured user-declared facts and authorised staff verification as the intended evidence workflow. Uploading a file, where a separate authorised workflow exists, does not itself verify the fact. Original RJSC/MoA/AoA document upload restrictions remain applicable.

## 6. Release gate

No rule should be represented as legally validated or statutory-compliance-certified merely because it is present in the runtime engine, canonical registry, seed file, applicability registry or evidence registry. Keep unresolved rules in REVIEW_REQUIRED / NOT_EVALUATED. Product-owner approval and NLC scoring policy do not substitute for independent legal approval.

**Next implementation step:** extract and test the exact runtime behaviour for each of the 85 rules against the canonical rule contract. Only after that comparison should specific runtime defects be patched. No legal proposition, exception or consequence should be changed solely to make the two code paths match.
