# Source review batch 2 — company filings, tax, VAT, securities, labour and stamp duty
Date: 13 November 2026

## Scope and status

This is a source-reconciliation workpaper for the remaining source-dependent rule families in the 85-rule catalogue. It is not a legal opinion and does not authorize seed changes, database writes, activation, scoring, penalties, or calendar events.

The controlling hierarchy is: promulgated Gazette / official Laws of Bangladesh text; current official regulator directions and forms for filing administration; professional/secondary commentary only as corroboration. The source version must be selected by the event date and the law's commencement/effective provision, not merely by the latest page available.

## Confirmed source findings and blockers

| Rule family | Current source evidence | Engineering/legal disposition | Still required |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR-001 — Schedule X annual return | RJSC's official FAQ says Schedule X (annual summary of share capital and list of shareholders/directors) is due within 21 days of the AGM under Companies Act s.36. | Keep separate from balance sheet / profit-and-loss filing. The rule must use AGM event date, verified Schedule X filing evidence and any applicable relief; do not infer non-filing from a missing upload. | Reconcile current s.36 text and prescribed Schedule X content; distinguish private/public company scope and historical filing records. |
| AR-002 / AR-003 — annual-return backlog | Companies Act s.346 is a separate Registrar strike-off procedure; RJSC filing backlog by itself is not proof that proceedings began or the company was dissolved. | Keep as internal escalation signals only. Never score statutory dissolution, director liability or BLACK from backlog count alone. | Evidence of Registrar notices/orders, service, response, restoration and current company status. |
| AR-004 — incomplete annual-return package | RJSC FAQ separately lists Schedule X within 21 days of AGM and balance sheet / profit-and-loss account within 30 days of AGM. | Do not collapse separate forms, documents and deadlines into a single binary "annual return complete" field. | Exact legal source and document-specific filing evidence; determine which attachments apply to the entity/class and period. |
| DIR-001 / DIR-002 — Form XII | RJSC FAQ identifies Form XII particulars/change filing within 14 days of appointment or change under s.115. The Act's s.115 also concerns the company's internal register of directors/managerial particulars. | Separate internal register maintenance from the Registrar filing. Separate filing default from validity/effective date of appointment, resignation, removal or death. | Verify current official English/Bangla s.115 and class scope; use per-director event and Form XII receipt, not a profile boolean. |
| DIR-007 — consent to act / Form IX | RJSC FAQ lists Form IX (consent to act) within 30 days of appointment under s.92. It separately lists Form XII under s.115 within 14 days. | The seed's s.93(2) mapping is not supported by the current RJSC FAQ. Do not conflate consent to act (Form IX) with particulars/change (Form XII). | Verify exact current s.92 and any amendment; confirm whether Form IX is required for the relevant company/director category and the trigger date. |
| SH-003 — increase of share capital / Form IV | RJSC FAQ lists Form IV notice of increase of share capital within 15 days under s.56. | Keep the filing duty separate from the underlying authority/resolution and the effective date of capital increase. | Verify exact s.56 trigger and any prescribed extension; evidence the resolution, effective capital event and Registrar receipt. |
| CAP-002 / CHG-001 — charges | RJSC FAQ lists Form XVIII particulars of mortgages/charges within 21 days under ss.159 and 391; Form XIX modification within 21 days under s.167(3) and 319; Form XXVIII satisfaction within 21 days under ss.12 and 391. | Rule IDs must distinguish creation, modification and satisfaction; only charges registrable under the applicable statutory provision are in scope. A missed upload is UNKNOWN. | Verify current statutory scope, registrability, event date, extension/late-registration route and current form; authenticate charge instrument and Registrar receipt. |
| BSEC-001 through BSEC-004 | BSEC's official Act/Rules/Regulations register lists the 2018 Corporate Governance Code, amendment dated 20-Nov-2023, and a 26-Jul-2026 direction extending the time for appointment of female independent directors in listed companies. | These rules are conditional on listed-issuer scope and the exact issuer class / applicable condition. They must not be applied to ordinary private limited companies. A single boolean cannot prove board composition or audit-committee compliance. | Retrieve the original Code and each applicable amendment/direction, verify effective dates, issuer class and current deadline, and collect board/committee records. |
| TAX-003 — company income-tax return | Official Laws of Bangladesh records Finance Act 2026 (Act 96 of 2026), including replacement of Income Tax Act ss.170 and 171. The official 2025 English consolidated Act predates those 2026 changes and showed a different Tax Day formulation. Secondary tax commentary reports a ninth-month company deadline after Finance Act 2026. | The seed's ninth-month formulation may reflect the 2026 amendment, but the 2025 English consolidated Act cannot be used as current law after the amendment. Do not implement from a secondary summary or stale Tax Day definition. | Obtain the exact Bengali text of Finance Act 2026 ss.97 and 99 and a reviewed translation; model company / bank / insurance / financial-company income-year classes, public-holiday rule, transitional year and extensions. |
| TAX-004 — advance tax | The official 2025 English consolidated Income Tax Act shows s.154(1) threshold of BDT 6 lakh and four instalments under s.155. Finance Act 2026 is listed by NBR and the seed says s.154 was amended by s.87; secondary sources report the threshold was raised to BDT 10 lakh. | The 2025 text is stale for this proposition. Do not use the old 6-lakh threshold or adopt the reported 10-lakh threshold without reviewing the enacted 2026 amendment. Advance tax is not a simple "missed quarter" boolean: eligibility, exclusions, A-B computation, source deductions, instalment schedule and holiday treatment matter. | Read Finance Act 2026 s.87 and the current Act text; verify taxpayer eligibility and exceptions, estimates, payments and credit/source-deduction records. |
| VAT-002 — periodic VAT return | Finance Act 2026 initially replaced VAT Act s.64 with a return after every three tax periods (effective 1-Jul-2026). The Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty (Amendment) Ordinance 2026, Ordinance 38, dated 14-Oct-2026, replaced s.64 again: return within 15 days after each tax period; if day 15 is a government holiday, next working day; government/semi-government/autonomous bodies, banks, insurance and zero-return filers may file within 20 days. | The operative rule changed during 2026. Version by tax-period date and commencement must be enforced. A fixed 15th-of-next-month rule is incomplete because of the 20-day classes and public-holiday adjustment. | Confirm ordinance commencement/effective date (the official consolidated Act footnote states 1-Oct-2026), taxpayer category, monthly tax period, valid NBR extension orders and current prescribed electronic form. |
| VAT-003 — annual VAT return | Current s.64 and NBR material support periodic returns; no universal annual return duty is established by the source review. | Keep NOT_EVALUATED / out of automated statutory conclusions until a specific taxpayer-class Act/Rule/SRO provision is identified. | Identify exact annual return provision, taxpayer class, period and form before defining a trigger. |
| TR-002 — share-transfer stamp duty | Stamp Act s.3 charges instruments listed in Schedule I. The Stamp Act Schedule I was replaced by Finance Act 2022. Schedule I Article 62(a) is titled transfer of shares in an incorporated company/body corporate. Section 62 of the Stamp Act is a separate penalty provision, not Schedule I Article 62. IRD lists a 2026 SRO (No.386-Law/2026) in its stamp-law materials. | The source must say Schedule I Article 62(a), not merely "section 62". Do not hard-code a rate from an old schedule or infer duty/invalidity from a missing receipt. | Retrieve current Schedule I as amended, the 2026 SRO and all relevant instruments; verify instrument category, execution date, consideration/value basis, exemptions, adjudication and payment evidence. |
| LBR-001 / LBR-002 — factory licence | Official Gazette lists Bangladesh Labour (Amendment) Act 2026, Act 43, dated 10-Apr-2026. The seed appropriately describes a Government direction under s.326 as a required condition. | No generic factory licence failure/expiry finding without the current s.326 text, Government direction, establishment class and licence category. | Reconcile authentic Bangla s.326 after Act 43, directions and prescribed licence terms; verify renewal application, term, extension and establishment scope. |
| LBR-003 — Labour Court order | A case-specific certified Labour Court order, not a generic Labour Act citation, is the operative source for an order-pending condition. | Review-only; no statutory breach from a reported pending boolean. | Certified order, parties, service, operative directions, appeal/revision/stay and current status. |
| TL-001 / TL-002 — trade licence | Seed cites City Corporation Act 2009 / Paurashava Act 2009 but the exact authority, provision, licence term and renewal cycle vary by local authority and business class. | The universal "renew by 31 March" statement is not established by the source set. Do not generate one national trade-licence deadline. | Map city corporation / municipality, local by-laws, trade category, licence document, validity dates and renewal notice/order. |
| INC-006 — foreign establishment remittance | The official Foreign Private Investment (Promotion and Protection) Act 1980 is a separate statute; Bangladesh Bank's current foreign-investment portal lists GFET-2018 and multiple later FE/FEID circulars. | Do not label a USD 50,000 remittance reference as a universal statutory requirement or attribute it to a misnamed "BIDA Foreign Investment Act". Applicability is conditional on entity/activity and current regulator guidance. | Identify exact BIDA guideline/circular, applicant category, effective date, zone and bank evidence; obtain current BIDA and Bangladesh Bank confirmation. |
