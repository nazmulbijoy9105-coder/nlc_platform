# Bangladesh legal source research

## Objective
Produce a source-grounded legal research record for Bangladesh corporate and RJSC compliance. Research is not a legal opinion and does not approve production rules.

## Required method
1. Identify the legal question, company type, relevant date, jurisdiction and decision context.
2. Search primary sources first: Bangladesh Gazette, enacted Acts, ordinances, statutory rules, official ministry/RJSC publications and authoritative court decisions.
3. Record exact instrument title, number, section/rule/article, publication date, commencement/effective date, amendment/repeal status, source URL or repository locator, access date and a content hash where available.
4. Distinguish enacted law from bills, draft rules, circulars, administrative guidance, commentary and secondary sources.
5. Check amendment chains and transitional provisions. Do not assume the latest consolidated text is complete.
6. Quote only the minimum necessary text; preserve exact wording for operative provisions in controlled source storage.
7. Identify conflicts, missing commencement evidence, unavailable gazettes, ambiguity and unresolved interpretive questions explicitly.
8. Provide a traceable proposition-to-source mapping and confidence limits.

## Output contract
Return:
- Question and as-of date
- Primary authorities, each with pinpoint citation and provenance
- Amendment/commencement timeline
- Legal proposition supported by each authority
- Secondary corroboration, separately labelled
- Conflicts, gaps and unresolved issues
- Researcher recommendation for independent Bangladesh counsel review

## Prohibitions
- Never invent a statute, section, rule, date, gazette number or URL.
- Never treat search snippets, AI summaries or unofficial reproductions as primary law.
- Never infer legal correctness from a passing software test.
- Never mark a rule legally verified or release-approved.
- If primary evidence is unavailable, report NOT ESTABLISHED rather than filling the gap.
